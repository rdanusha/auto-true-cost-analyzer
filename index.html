KNOW WHAT IS IN THE NUMBER

An estimate, not a prediction.

Five-year cost = depreciation + loan interest paid during five years + fuel + insurance + maintenance. Loan principal is not counted twice.

How the calculation works
  • Loans use fixed monthly payments, monthly interest, no fees, and no early repayments. Terms range from 24 to 84 months.
  • For loans longer than five years, remaining loan debt is included. Equivalently: down payment + payments made + remaining debt + running costs − estimated resale value.
  • Resale value falls by 20%, 15%, 12%, 10%, then 9% of the previous year's value. This generic model also applies to used cars and may not match your vehicle.
  • Year-one maintenance is multiplied by 1, 1.1, 1.25, 1.5, and 1.8 across five years.
  • Fuel price, mileage, and monthly insurance are held constant. Cost per mile is unavailable when annual mileage is zero.
  • Not included: sales tax, registration, dealer fees, parking, tolls, unexpected repairs outside your maintenance budget, inflation, opportunity cost, or EV charging. Add relevant costs separately.
Privacy and limitations

Calculations run in your browser. This app does not send your entries to a server, use analytics, or save entries in browser storage. The hosting provider still receives normal web requests and may log connection information. Closing or reloading the page clears your entries. This is an educational planning tool, not financial advice, a quote, or a guaranteed resale value.
